## 📊 Market Overview & Industry Dynamics

> **Estimated Sector Market Size**: The global Aviation Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul (MRO) market is valued at **$90 Billion to $105 Billion** (projected to reach over **$130 Billion by 2030**).  
> **Market Fragmentation**: The software and enterprise management sector for MRO is **highly concentrated** among a few category leaders (IFS Maintenix, AMOS, TRAX, Ramco, Veryon/CAMP) due to steep regulatory compliance hurdles (FAA, EASA, CAAC), complex airworthiness directives tracking, and deep integration requirements with airline operations and legacy ERP systems.

### 💡 Frameworks & Open-Source Considerations
- **Commercial vs. Open-Source**: Full-featured airline-grade aviation MRO platforms with airworthiness compliance, complex component rotables tracking, and regulatory depth (FAA/EASA) are almost exclusively commercial due to strict certification requirements.
- **Emerging Aircraft Segments**: Open-source efforts are strongest in drone/eVTOL fleet maintenance, structural health monitoring ML models, and general CMMS platforms adaptable for light non-certified aircraft.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated list** — provided for informational and research purposes only.
