# Awesome-Maintenance-Repair-n-Overhaul

## Top Maintenance Repair & Overhaul (MRO) Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Aviation MRO, Aircraft Maintenance Tracking, Technical Records, Work Orders, Airworthiness Compliance & Component Management*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS / commercial platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Maintenance, Repair & Overhaul (MRO)**—with primary emphasis on **aviation MRO**. These systems manage aircraft maintenance planning and execution, technical records, work orders, component/rotables tracking, airworthiness directives, inventory, and regulatory compliance for airlines, MROs, and operators.



**Examples** include IFS Maintenix, Ramco Aviation, AMOS (Swiss-AS), TRAX, Veryon, Rusada, CAMP Systems, eMRO, Flatirons, and Flydocs (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured aviation MRO platforms with airworthiness compliance, complex component tracking, and regulatory depth are almost exclusively commercial. Open-source activity is limited to drone/eVTOL-focused MRO tools, academic prototypes, regional aviation maintenance systems, and general CMMS that can be adapted. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[IFS Maintenix](https://www.ifs.com/)**  

  Leading aviation MRO and maintenance management platform covering planning, execution, engineering, logistics, and asset management for airlines and MRO providers.



- **[AMOS (Swiss-AS)](https://www.swiss-as.com/)**  

  Widely adopted aircraft maintenance and engineering system used by airlines and MROs for technical records, work packaging, and operational compliance.



- **[TRAX / TRAX eMRO](https://www.trax.aero/)**  

  Comprehensive aviation maintenance software for work packages, technical records, inventory, and fleet maintenance control.



- **[Ramco Aviation](https://www.ramco.com/)**  

  Integrated aviation suite covering aircraft maintenance, engineering, supply chain, finance, and workforce management for airlines and MROs.



- **[Veryon, Rusada, CAMP Systems](https://www.veryon.com/)**  

  Solutions focused on maintenance tracking, technical records, continuing airworthiness, and digital aircraft documentation.



- **[eMRO, Flatirons, Flydocs](https://www.flydocs.com/)**  

  Platforms supporting MRO operations, digital technical records, document management, and airworthiness data for aviation organizations.



- **[Other commercial aviation MRO & maintenance platforms](https://www.ifs.com/)**  

  Additional systems for line and base maintenance, component tracking, and regulatory compliance in commercial and business aviation.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Emerging aircraft segments**: Drone/eVTOL MRO tools as the most domain-specific open efforts currently available.

- **Prototypes & research**: Academic systems exploring inspection, damage detection, and maintenance lifecycle concepts.

- **General CMMS**: Open maintenance platforms adapted carefully for non-certified or light-aircraft contexts.

- **Composable stacks**: Inventory + work orders + document store + basic compliance checklists for very small operators or research fleets.

- Full airline/MRO-grade systems with complex airworthiness, configuration control, and multi-fleet scale remain commercial.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

There is no mature, production-ready open-source equivalent to IFS Maintenix, AMOS, TRAX, Ramco Aviation, Veryon, or similar commercial aviation MRO platforms.  

The strongest domain-specific open efforts today target **drones and eVTOL**. General CMMS, document management, and workflow tools can support limited or experimental use cases.  

Commercial aviation MRO software provides regulatory alignment (e.g., continuing airworthiness, technical records integrity), complex component/rotable tracking, integration with flight operations and ERP, and the auditability required by airlines and approved maintenance organizations.  

Most commercial operators and MROs rely on established commercial platforms; open-source is best suited for emerging vehicle types, research, training, or highly customized internal tools under strict airworthiness oversight.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Aviation maintenance is safety- and regulation-critical. Systems handling technical records, airworthiness data, or release-to-service processes must meet applicable aviation authority requirements. Incorrect or incomplete records can have serious safety and legal consequences.

- Open-source tools may support learning, prototyping, or non-certified applications but generally lack the validation, configuration control, and regulatory maturity of commercial aviation MRO platforms. Do not use unvalidated software for certified aircraft maintenance without thorough regulatory and engineering review.
